## Not backed by a record (summary)

- `assets/sim-bimanual/tools/make_manifests.py` and the frozen/swap/eval lines of the README's step 4b, 4c and 5 that call it: reconstructions from the rules the records state, not published commands.
- Row names of the run10 swap manifest for pairs 2 to 9: the record states the pairing rule and the row count (20) and names only rows 0 and 1 (shared with run09); the other names follow run09's `swap_<frame>_actions_<actions>` pattern.
- The first action text of the prompt for episodes other than 815362 and 810449: the record says "task name + first annotated action text" per episode; only those two prompts are quoted verbatim.
- The `--revision` flags on the `hf download` commands: added as pins; the records ran the downloads without them and recorded the resolved revisions.
