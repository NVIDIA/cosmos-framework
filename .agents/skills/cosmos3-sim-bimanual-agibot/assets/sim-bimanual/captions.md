# Assets — Cosmos3-Nano-Sim-Bimanual recipe (held-out AgiBotWorld-Beta, one GB300)

All frames are from the 901-frame rollouts described in the README: checkpoint `nvidia/Cosmos3-Nano-Sim-Bimanual` revision `a9e3adf6235f56f0082be7a96e497348c286c472`, forward dynamics, 4 steps, guidance 3.0, shift 5.0, seed 42, KV cache 64, attention sink 8, 640x352 output, 30 fps. "Real" is the AgiBotWorld-Beta head camera resized to 640x360 as in training; "matched-action rollout" replays the episode's own actions (schema 5, `normalized_unified_v1`) from its real first frame; "frozen first frame" holds the real frame 0 for all 901 frames (static control). The pictures and the GIF were made by `tools/make_media.py`.

**Source data, licence and attribution.** The "real" panels and every first frame in these pictures and clips are from the dataset `agibot-world/AgiBotWorld-Beta` (AgiBot World Contributors; Hugging Face, gated), dataset revision `2252b3fdc88dc67fa5d5e4b771e1b86f5e71c278`, licensed **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** under the "AgiBot World COMMUNITY LICENSE AGREEMENT" shown on the dataset card. The media in this folder are derivative works of that dataset (crops, frame strips and side-by-side composites with model output) and are shared under the same licence, for non-commercial use with attribution; the generated panels are synthetic and must be labelled as such before they enter any dataset. Episodes shown: 815362 (task 600) and 810449 (task 596). The recipe does not bundle or rehost any original dataset file; users download the dataset from the publisher after accepting its terms.

**Crop used for every comparison ("top 352 rows"):** the real 640x360 frame is cropped to rows 0–351 and all 640 columns; the bottom 8 rows are discarded. `tools/eval_fidelity.py --align auto` chose this "top" crop for every matched, frozen and swap comparison by frame-0 PSNR (task600_ep815362: top 35.55 dB, center 21.25 dB, bottom 18.57 dB), recorded in each `*_summary.json` as `real_crop_box`. The pictures apply the same rows.
